How It Works

A smarter path to
cognitive clarity

BrainCompass guides patients through clinically validated, adaptive question sequences that intelligently adjust difficulty based on real-time responses.

1

Patient Onboarding

A clinician or caregiver sets up the assessment on any device in under 2 minutes — no training required.

2

Adaptive Q&A Session

BrainCompass presents multi-domain questions covering memory, orientation, language and reasoning — adapting difficulty dynamically.

3

AI-Driven Analysis

Our engine scores responses against clinical baselines (MoCA, MMSE) and longitudinal patient data in real time.

4

Clinician Report

A detailed PDF report with cognitive domain breakdowns, risk flags, and trend graphs is instantly available in your EHR.
